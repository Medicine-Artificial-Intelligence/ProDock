CHEMICAL USE-CASE ANALYSIS PROTOCOL — FIXED BEFORE CAMPAIGN RESULTS

Scope: descriptive noncovalent WT-EGFR recognition and pose-inspection workflow,
not an affinity predictor, covalent docking protocol, activity benchmark or
prospective screening validation. ChEMBL37 IC50 annotations summarize diverse
assays and their median pIC50 values are contextual annotations only. No activity
labels select weights, metrics, thresholds, poses, pocket radius or examples.

Input: frozen ChEMBL203 library; all available valid rank-1 docking poses,
actual ProLIF interaction-event rows and context with prepared protein/reference
paths. Primary engine is smina. Each receptor/program channel retains its own
best-scored pose; no alternative pose is chosen by activity labels or hinge bond.
Additional engines are descriptive cross-checks, never selected by favorable
outcome. Missing results are counted; paired comparisons use complete pairs only.

Questions and fixed analyses
1. Structure dependence: paired compound rank percentiles (tied average ranks,
   lower score better), Spearman rank correlation, absolute percentile shift,
   and top-decile overlap between the two protein structures for each engine.
   Top-decile means rank <= ceil(n_complete_pairs*0.10); ties are kept and actual
   selected-set counts are reported. This is rank stability, not accuracy.
2. Hinge recognition: actual ProLIF HBAcceptor/HBDonor events at prepared MET98
   in 1M17 or MET87 in 4ZAU. Separately flag any contact event at that residue.
   Tabulate hinge contact/H-bond counts and fractions by heterogeneous activity
   stratum, and retain the residue mapping. Absence is a pose-event observation,
   not proof that the molecule cannot bind. Missing profiling is not zero.
3. Size/flexibility: plot score versus heavy-atom count and report Spearman
   associations with heavy-atom count, rotatable bonds and median pIC50. Also
   report -docking_score/heavy_atoms as a size diagnostic, explicitly not an
   experimental ligand-efficiency estimate or a newly validated ranking score.
4. Contact-size sensitivity: before reading outcomes, define native-ligand
   residue neighborhoods from prepared protein heavy atoms within 5.0 A of each
   experimental reference ligand. Align prepared protein sequences to the first
   prepared EGFR structure and map residues to a common anchor identity. The
   common pocket is the UNION of those mapped native neighborhoods. Primary
   radius is 5.0 A; 6.0 and 8.0 A are fixed sensitivity radii. Compare historical
   per-template set Tanimoto with common-pocket coverage = contacted pocket
   residues / available common-pocket residues. Extra contacts outside the
   pocket do not lower coverage, but coverage can favor larger compounds; report
   its size association. This is an explicit diagnostic, not proof of a better
   scoring function. Preserve residue mapping and pocket lists for inspection.
5. Inspection shortlist: at most three per category, selected without activity
   labels. (a) stable_hinge: top decile in both primary-engine structures AND a
   hinge H-bond in both; sort worst percentile, then mean percentile, then ID.
   (b) good_score_no_hinge: mean percentile <=0.10 AND no hinge H-bond in either
   adequately profiled pose; sort mean percentile then ID.
   (c) structure_sensitive: absolute percentile shift >=0.50; sort shift descending,
   mean percentile, then ID. Do not relax thresholds or fill empty categories.
   Export scores, ranks, contacts, structural descriptors, activity annotations
   and reasons. Draw 2D structures for these rule-selected cases only.
6. Annotate a generic acrylamide substructure SMARTS C=CC(=O)N. This flag does not
   establish electrophilic reactivity, covalent activity or selectivity. The
   4ZAU reference AZD9291 has an acrylamide, but this study models only noncovalent
   recognition and predicts neither reaction chemistry nor mutant selectivity.

Figures: paired rank percentiles; actual hinge H-bond frequencies by annotated
stratum; score-versus-size; pocket-overlap/coverage size diagnostic. Export all
underlying compound-level tables and correlation counts. Statistical summaries
are descriptive; no p-values will be used to claim general predictive ability.
